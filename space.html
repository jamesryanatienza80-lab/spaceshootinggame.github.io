📖 HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Move your mouse or finger to steer the ship. Hold the left mouse button or touch the screen to fire.

SURVIVE

Meteors deal 10 hull damage and boss bullets or direct boss collisions deal 20. Shields block one hit per charge; hull health reaching zero ends the run.

UPGRADES

Destroy glowing yellow upgrade drops and choose one of three upgrades. Most upgrades stack up to Level 3.

BOSSES

Mini Bosses begin at 100 points, regular Bosses at 500 points, and Final Bosses at 1,000 points. Boss victories award bonus points.
